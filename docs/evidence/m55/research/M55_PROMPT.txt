## 2. Extend the liquid system, not a second water universe

Add an optional dynamic-surface capability to appropriate M54 reservoirs. Static equilibrium mode remains supported. Existing non-opted-in M54 content and legacy PBF behaviour must remain valid.

For an active reservoir:

    reservoir.volume = sum(surfaceCell.volume)

Cell quantities partition the SAME reservoir's liquid. Do not add them again to system accounting:

    total = sum(reservoirs) + sum(containers) + sum(detached parcels)

Use one mutation/transaction path. Internal face flow changes the partition, not the reservoir total. External transfers update the local allocation and ownership totals together, exactly once. No independently writable competing totals.

Preserve M54 double-precision quantity accounting and its separation between ledger tolerance, geometric approximation and numerical-solve residual. Never repair geometry by editing the expected total or inventing/deleting litres.

M54's whole-basin equilibrium inversion is an initializer/reference, not an instruction to flatten the moving surface every step.

## 3. Surface geometry and bake

Build local simulation cells and their connectivity from the existing physical cavity, not render meshes or bounding-box volume estimates. A whole-basin capacity curve alone is insufficient.

Each connected cell needs:
- local storage/capacity versus equilibrium coordinate;
- geometry/metric information;
- shared faces with wetted aperture, distance and barrier/opening information;
- explicit closed boundaries and M54 connection associations.

Conceptually, available storage is:

    V_i = C_i(q_i, solidGeometry)

where q is the supported plane/radial coordinate. Invert local storage for the local surface level. Preserve physical wet/dry occupancy and shoreline geometry.

Do not join disconnected pockets merely because they fall inside one coarse cell. Split/refine that cell or reject unsupported geometry clearly. No flow through walls or across dry saddles by global averaging.

Use fixed-resolution modest surface meshes initially. A small basin and a swimming pool use the same capability at appropriate resolution. No vertical dynamic layers. Offline tetrahedral geometry is fine; evolving the deep interior is not.

Reuse editor/resource baking, stable asset IDs, source/settings fingerprints and stale startup/export rejection. Do not rebuild all static geometry each runtime frame. Preserve accepted M54 assets; new surface data must not invalidate historical evidence.

## 4. Numerical method

Before broad integration, record the actual discretization: state variables, gravity/flow update, wetting/drying treatment, boundary rules, timestep conditions, iteration limits and residuals. The research equations are a design starting point, not a complete numerical proof.

Orient every shared face once. Calculate one signed discharge Q_f and use the identical accepted transfer on both sides:

    delta = dt * Q_f
    cellA.volume -= delta
    cellB.volume += delta

Gravity and surface gradients must evolve flow, producing propagation/reflection rather than just smoothing heights. Use a coherent semi-implicit surface gravity/continuity step, with a documented low-order transport/friction treatment where needed.

Requirements:
- calm water over irregular terrain remains at rest;
- nonnegative liquid through the chosen numerical method;
- shared transfers remain conservative during wetting/drying;
- moving waves survive legitimate drain/refill operations;
- no uncontrolled energy growth in ordinary disturbance/body fixtures;
- finite, bounded solve cost and useful failure diagnostics.

A method-compatible conservative outgoing-flux limiter is permitted. Post-update negative-volume clamping, hidden global rescaling, scene-specific fixes and a fictitious floor just below the surface are not.

Semi-implicit integration is intended to avoid an explicit gravity-wave timestep restriction driven solely by sqrt(g*depth). It does not guarantee accurate deep-water waves, constant iteration counts or identical runtime at all depths. Measure damping/propagation as well as stability. No silent depth cap to make a benchmark pass.

Use bounded retry/substeps if necessary, with coherent rollback of affected state/transactions. Never partially commit a failed solve or silently replace failed dynamics with decorative waves. If the small core proof repeatedly fails, stop with the reproducer and design issue rather than accumulating patches while expanding the demo.

Keep gravity direction, wave-surface normal and solid-contact normal separate. Preserve fixed double absolute origin plus local simulation coordinates; no live rebasing. Whole-planet streaming, zero-g blobs and unsupported changing/nonconservative equilibrium fields remain outside scope.

## 7. Rigid-body displacement and loading

A body entering water removes available SPACE, not liquid mass.

Use actual supported collider geometry to update affected local storage and relevant apertures. Unchanged liquid quantity must redistribute/rise around the body. Do not inject litres as a shortcut for a splash.

Avoid double subtraction of overlapping solids. Hollow buckets are not solid bounding hulls. Container ownership exclusions and M54 scoop transactions must agree: their contents cannot simultaneously be reservoir water and container water.

Extend the existing LiquidInteraction sampling/loading path to use the dynamic occupied region and modeled flow. Apply buoyancy/drag once through PhysicsWorld. Preserve the existing legacy-PBF ownership guard. No second full buoyancy force from M55.

Document fixed-step ordering between rigid poses, geometry changes, liquid transfers/solve, forces and rigid integration. Do not tick physics twice. Moving-boundary coupling is a specific implementation risk; prove moderate-speed entry/exit and horizontal motion before scaling up the demo.

This is approximate hydrostatic/depth-reduced coupling, not exact combined liquid/body momentum or energy conservation. No velocity clipping merely to hide an unstable coupling. Existing container mass/inertia limitations must remain explicit unless separately justified work is needed; do not turn M55 into that redesign.

## 8. Cups, pouring and a bounded splash path
